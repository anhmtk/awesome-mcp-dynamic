# Code of conduct

This list is a public workshop. People will arrive with servers, objections, and half-formed requests. Treat them as collaborators.

## Expected

- Comment on the work. Be specific. Say what you read and what you think it should say instead.
- Assume the other person is trying to make the pattern clearer.
- Credit the source you are adding. Do not present someone else's server or essay as your own.
- Keep secrets out of issues, pull requests, and examples. Redact tickets before they are published.
- Accept a "no" on scope. Tool loading, price feeds, and general MCP directories have other homes.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, slurs, or personal attacks
- Spam listings, referral dumps, or repeated posts of the same product
- Publishing someone else's credentials, customer data, or private messages
- Pressure on the maintainer to merge a listing that skips human review and runs client-supplied code

## Reports

Open an issue if the report can be public. For a private report, email [admin@agentshare.dev](mailto:admin@agentshare.dev). The maintainer will reply with what will happen next. The address is for conduct and for sensitive reports about this repository, not for product support.
